About the firm

Major-firm expertise. A boutique's flexibility and access.

Our firm was founded in 2012 by former partners from Jones Day, one of the nation's premier law firms, to provide top-quality work in an intimate, relationship-oriented environment. With the expertise and business perspective gained from decades at one of the country's largest firms, we work with a diverse range of clients — corporations, closely held companies, partnerships, LLCs, and the individuals who own them.

We have practiced together for a long time, and we value that partnership. In a boutique practice like ours, we can be more flexible, less bureaucratic, and highly accessible to our clients — while providing major-firm expertise. Our primary focus is partnering with clients to understand their needs, so we function as key advisors to their businesses.
